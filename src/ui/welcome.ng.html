Connect your Fender Mustang amplifier via USB to edit presets, configure signal chains, and access hidden amp models directly in your browser without installing legacy software.

This is an independent open-source hobby project created to keep the Mustang amplifier series alive on modern systems. It has no affiliation with, and is not endorsed by, Fender Musical Instruments Corporation.
